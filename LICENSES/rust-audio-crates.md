# Rust audio crates

Linked into the app for the stem player and stem engine (decoding, time stretching, resampling, WAV I/O, FFT, Core ML), with every normal dependency. Generated by `scripts/collect-audio-licenses.py`; the license texts are in [`rust-audio-crates.txt`](rust-audio-crates.txt). Where a crate offers a choice, the app uses it under the license in the "Used" column.

Symphonia is MPL-2.0: the app uses its files unmodified, and their source is at <https://github.com/pdeljanov/Symphonia> (tag `v0.6.1`) and on crates.io.
