Generated by `scripts/collect-licenses.py`; the license texts are in [`rust-crates.txt`](rust-crates.txt). Where a crate offers a choice, the app uses it under the license in the "Used" column.

The MPL-2.0 crates (Symphonia, `cssparser`, `selectors` and the others with MPL-2.0 in the "Used" column) are used unmodified; their source is at the repository in the "Source" column and on crates.io.
